Set up in 3 minutes.

Oso isn't on the Play Store, so you install it straight from here.

  1. Download

    Tap Download APK on your phone.

  2. Install

    Open the file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.

    Play Protect may say “unknown app”. Tap More details → Install anyway.

  3. Add an AI key

    Open Settings, pick a provider, paste your key, tap Test connection.

    No key yet? Get a free one.

  4. Start learning

    Type a topic, choose how long, tap Start learning. Your first cards are ready in a minute or two.

Pick your AI.

OpenRouter

Free · Best start

One key, hundreds of models. The default picks a free one for you.

  1. Sign in at openrouter.ai
  2. Tap Create key and copy it
  3. Paste it in Oso under OpenRouter

Default model openrouter/free

Private by design.

No account. No server. Your learning stays on your phone.

  • No account

    No sign-up, email or password. Open the app and start.

  • Your key, locked

    Encrypted by Android's Keystore. Never logged, never backed up, never shown in errors.

  • Data on your device

    Topics, cards, chats and streak live in a database on your phone. Oso has no server.

  • No tracking

    No analytics, ads or crash reporters. Nobody watches how you learn.

  • One connection

    The app talks straight to your AI provider. No middleman.

Stays on your phone

  • Your API key
  • Topics, cards and progress
  • Your streak and reminders

Sent to your AI provider

  • Your topic and chat messages
  • Text from a file, if you add one

Only when writing cards or chatting. Their privacy policy applies.

  • Set a spending limit on paid keys. Revoke a key any time from your provider's dashboard.
  • Delete a topic to erase it. Uninstall to erase everything; there's no copy anywhere else.

Questions

Is Oso free?

Yes. With a free provider, you pay nothing. With a paid one, you pay them directly, per use.

Is installing an APK safe?

Install only from this site. Android warns about any app outside the Play Store; that warning is expected.

Can I move to a new phone?

Not yet. Your data never leaves your phone, so it doesn't come along. Your key isn't backed up either; paste it again.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once a topic is ready. Only writing new cards needs internet.

What if I miss a day?

Your streak survives one missed day. Sessions aren't tied to dates, so nothing piles up.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Oso is Android only for now.

Reminders don't show up.

Some phones (Xiaomi, Tecno, Infinix) pause apps to save battery. Turn on Auto-start for Oso and set battery to No restrictions.
